# Weekly update intent — implemented

See `README.md` for the current system guide and implemented architecture.

## Purpose

Run a simple, consistent weekly update for the family stock trading game. The existing dashboard stays exactly as it is.

## Weekly timing and prices

- The trading cut-off is Thursday night.
- Use the Thursday-night prices as the official prices for that week's update.
- At 08:05 local time every Friday, calculate the standings, update the website, and publish it automatically.

## Publication and email notification

- Publish the prepared website update automatically; no approval step is required.
- Email Michael when the update has been published.
- Put the WhatsApp-ready message at the top of the email so Michael can copy it into the family WhatsApp group from his phone.
- Include a link to the updated website for reference.
- Do not add an approval page or make any changes to the family dashboard beyond the normal weekly update.
- If the update cannot be completed safely, do not publish a partial or stale update; email Michael a short explanation instead.
- The email may arrive before the public website has finished refreshing.

## WhatsApp message

Once the update is ready, provide Michael with a short message ready to copy into the family WhatsApp group. It should contain only:

1. The leaderboard.
2. The biggest weekly movers.
3. One brief, good-natured playful comment about the week's action.

Keep the tone warm, concise, and suitable for the whole family. Do not add news, extra awards, market analysis, reminders, or new dashboard features unless asked.

## Example format

```text
📈 Family Stock Challenge — Week [number]

🏆 Leaderboard
1. [Name] — £[portfolio value] ([overall return])
2. [Name] — £[portfolio value] ([overall return])
3. [Name] — £[portfolio value] ([overall return])

🚀 This week's movers
• [Name]: [share/portfolio] [up/down] [weekly change]
• [Name]: [share/portfolio] [up/down] [weekly change]

[One short playful, kind comment.]
```
